# Tomato Disease Management

Tomato diseases include early blight, late blight, bacterial wilt and viral leaf curl. Use resistant varieties where available, remove infected plants, control whiteflies for leaf curl, ensure drainage, rotate crops and use need-based fungicides.

Practical chatbot answer guidance: Ask for crop, location, season, soil type and symptoms before giving a final recommendation. Prefer preventive, low-risk and locally verified practices.
