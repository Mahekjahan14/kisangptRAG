# Rice Nutrient Management

Apply nitrogen in splits rather than one heavy dose. Combine farmyard manure, phosphorus, potassium, zinc where deficient, and site-specific nutrient management. Excess nitrogen encourages pest attack and lodging. Leaf color charts can help decide nitrogen top dressing.

Practical chatbot answer guidance: Ask for crop, location, season, soil type and symptoms before giving a final recommendation. Prefer preventive, low-risk and locally verified practices.
