# Rice Nursery Management

Prepare a raised or wet nursery with treated seed. Sow pre-germinated seed uniformly and protect nursery from water stagnation and pests. Seedlings are usually transplanted when vigorous and young. Avoid dense nursery because weak seedlings increase disease risk and reduce tillering after transplanting.
