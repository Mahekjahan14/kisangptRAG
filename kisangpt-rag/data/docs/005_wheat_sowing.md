# Wheat Sowing and Varieties

Wheat performs best with timely sowing in cool weather, well-prepared seedbed, quality seed, and recommended spacing. Delayed sowing reduces yield, so short-duration or heat-tolerant varieties are preferred when sowing late. Seed treatment reduces loose smut and seed-borne diseases.
