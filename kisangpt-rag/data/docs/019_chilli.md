# Chilli Crop Management

Chilli requires nursery care, balanced nutrients and regular monitoring for thrips, mites, fruit borer and viral diseases. Mulching can reduce weeds and conserve moisture. Avoid indiscriminate pesticide sprays because pest resurgence and residues may increase.

Practical chatbot answer guidance: Ask for crop, location, season, soil type and symptoms before giving a final recommendation. Prefer preventive, low-risk and locally verified practices.
