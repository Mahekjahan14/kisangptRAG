# Fertilizer Calculation

Fertilizer recommendations are given as nutrient quantities such as N, P2O5 and K2O. Convert them to fertilizer products based on nutrient percentage. Split nitrogen and apply phosphorus and potassium based on soil test and crop need.

Practical chatbot answer guidance: Ask for crop, location, season, soil type and symptoms before giving a final recommendation. Prefer preventive, low-risk and locally verified practices. When recommending chemicals or scheme benefits, ask the farmer to confirm current label or official state guidance.
