# Drip Irrigation

Drip irrigation supplies water near the root zone and reduces evaporation and weed growth. It suits vegetables, orchards, cotton and sugarcane. Filters must be cleaned regularly. Fertigation through drip improves nutrient efficiency when schedules are followed.
