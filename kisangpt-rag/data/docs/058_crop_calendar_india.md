# Crop Calendar India

A structured crop calendar ensures timely agronomic operations to secure healthy yields. Sowing, weeding, fertilizer application, irrigation, and harvesting windows must align with seasonal markers:

- **Rice (Kharif):** Nursery sowing in June, transplanting in July (25-30 days seedlings), vegetative weeding in August, flowering in September-October, harvesting in November.
- **Cotton:** Sowing in May-June, thinning and weeding in July, squaring and flowering in August-September, boll development in October-November, picking/harvest in December-January.
- **Wheat (Rabi):** Land preparation and sowing in November, first irrigation (Crown Root Initiation CRI) in December, vegetative and flowering in January-February, grain filling in March, harvesting in April.
- **Bengal Gram (Chickpea):** Sowing in October-November, flowering in January, pod development in February, harvesting in March-April.
