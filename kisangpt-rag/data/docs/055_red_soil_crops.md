# Red Soil Crops and Management

Red soils cover a large part of peninsular India, characterized by their reddish color due to high iron oxide content. Physically, they are light-textured, well-drained, sandy-clay-loam soils with low water-holding capacity. Chemically, they are slightly acidic, poor in nitrogen, organic matter, phosphorus, and zinc.

Suitable crops for red soil suitability include Groundnut (highly recommended), Pigeon Pea (Redgram), Finger Millet (Ragi), Castor, Cowpea, Chillies, Turmeric, Bajra, and horticultural crops like Mango and Custard Apple. 

Management practices:
- Incorporate organic manures, farmyard manure (FYM), or compost to improve water retention and soil organic carbon.
- Correct phosphorus deficiency through single super phosphate (SSP) application.
- Apply zinc sulfate to alleviate micronutrient deficiencies.
- Practice mulching to conserve moisture in dryland farming conditions.
