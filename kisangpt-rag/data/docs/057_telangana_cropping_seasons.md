# Telangana and India Cropping Seasons

Cropping patterns in India and Telangana are primarily divided into three distinct agro-climatic seasons:

1. **Kharif (Monsoon / Vanakalam):**
   - **Period:** June to October.
   - **Key Crops:** Rice, Cotton, Maize, Soybeans, Redgram (Pigeon Pea), Groundnut, Jowar.
   - **Advisory:** Sowing starts with the onset of southwest monsoon. Water conservation and weed management are crucial.

2. **Rabi (Winter / Yasangi):**
   - **Period:** November to April.
   - **Key Crops:** Wheat, Bengal Gram (Chickpea), Yasangi Rice (in Telangana under wells/canals), Mustard, Groundnut, Barley.
   - **Advisory:** Rely on residual soil moisture and irrigation. Maintain temperature-sensitive irrigation for wheat.

3. **Zaid (Summer):**
   - **Period:** March to June.
   - **Key Crops:** Moong (Green Gram), Fodder Crops, Watermelon, Cucumber, Pumpkin, Leafy Vegetables.
   - **Advisory:** Requires intensive irrigation; focus on short-duration crops.
