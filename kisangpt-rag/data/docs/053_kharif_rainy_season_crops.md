# Kharif Rainy Season Crops

The Kharif or rainy season cropping begins with the onset of the southwest monsoon in June-July and wraps up with harvesting in September-October. Key rainy season crops in India include Rice, Maize, Sorghum, Pearl Millet (Bajra), Finger Millet (Ragi), Soybeans, Groundnuts, Pigeon Pea (Redgram), and Cotton. 

These crops require high temperatures, warm humid weather, and substantial water for their growth cycle. Monsoon timings dictate sowing dates; delayed monsoons require short-duration varieties. Critical management practices involve field leveling for uniform water distribution, robust drainage to prevent waterlogging, timely weed control before the critical crop-weed competition period, and integrated pest management (IPM) for early-stage crop protection.
