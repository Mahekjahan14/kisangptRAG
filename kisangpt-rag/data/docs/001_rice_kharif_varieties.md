# Rice Kharif Varieties

Rice needs warm humid climate, standing water during early growth, and timely transplanting. Use locally recommended varieties, healthy seedlings, line transplanting, and balanced fertilizers. In rainfed areas choose short-duration varieties. Maintain field bunds to conserve water and monitor blast, sheath blight, stem borer and brown planthopper.
