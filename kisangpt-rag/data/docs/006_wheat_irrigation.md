# Wheat Irrigation Scheduling

Critical irrigation stages in wheat include crown root initiation, tillering, jointing, flowering, milking and dough stages. Crown root initiation is especially important. Avoid water stress during flowering and grain filling because it reduces grain number and grain weight.
