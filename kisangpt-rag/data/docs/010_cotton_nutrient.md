# Cotton Nutrient Management

Cotton requires balanced nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium. Organic manure improves soil structure. Excess nitrogen delays maturity and increases sucking pests. Micronutrients such as boron, zinc or magnesium should be applied only where deficiency symptoms or soil test indicate.
