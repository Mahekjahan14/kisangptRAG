# Maize Production Practices

Maize needs good drainage, uniform plant population, timely weeding and adequate nitrogen. Sowing on ridges can help in heavy soils. Fall armyworm monitoring is essential from early whorl stage. Balanced fertilization and irrigation at tasseling and silking improve yield.
