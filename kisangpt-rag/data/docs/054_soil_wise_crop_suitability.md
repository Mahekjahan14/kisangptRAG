# Soil-wise Crop Suitability

Understanding soil-wise crop suitability is fundamental to maximizing farm yield and maintaining soil health. Major Indian soils exhibit unique structures and nutrient levels:

1. **Alluvial Soils:** Highly fertile, rich in potash, well-suited for Rice, Wheat, Sugarcane, Jute, Pulses, and Oilseeds.
2. **Black Soils (Regur):** Clayey, excellent water-holding capacity, highly suitable for Cotton, Soybeans, Wheat, Bengal Gram, Citrus fruits, and Rabi Jowar.
3. **Red Soils:** Sandy-clay structure, well-drained, suited for Groundnuts, Redgram, Finger Millet (Ragi), Potato, Chillies, Tobacco, and Orchards under proper irrigation.
4. **Laterite Soils:** Acidic, low fertility, ideal for plantation crops like Cashew, Tea, Coffee, and Rubber.
5. **Sandy/Desert Soils:** High permeability, low water-holding capacity, suitable for drought-resistant crops like Bajra (Pearl Millet), Guar, and Moth Bean.
