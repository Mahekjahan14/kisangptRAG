# Rice Pest Management

For rice pests, combine field sanitation, resistant varieties, light traps, pheromone traps, need-based spraying, and conservation of natural enemies. Brown planthopper increases with excess nitrogen and close spacing. Stem borer dead hearts should be monitored early; rogue heavily affected tillers.
