# Cotton Sowing Window

Cotton should be sown with adequate soil moisture and suitable temperature. Avoid off-season sowing where heat and water stress increase red disease and pest pressure. Use certified seed, refuge rows for Bt cotton, and maintain plant population through gap filling.
